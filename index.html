About R.O.D

Every space begins at its roots.

R.O.D – Roots of Design is an architecture and interior design studio. We design private homes, commercial buildings, interiors and landscapes for clients in India and abroad.

We start by understanding the site, the climate and how you will use the space. That understanding shapes every plan, material and detail, so the finished project feels right for the people who live and work in it. One team takes your project from the first sketch to the final handover.

Our services
Vision

Spaces that last

To be a studio whose work is valued for its clarity, comfort and permanence.

Mission

Design with care

To turn each client’s needs into well-planned, well-built spaces, delivered honestly and on time.

Philosophy

Less, but better

Natural light, honest materials and good proportion matter more than decoration.

Approach

Listen, then design

We study the brief and the site, test ideas in 3D and refine every detail before work begins on site.
